## ⚡ Quick Answers

### "Is CARBS production-ready?"
**Yes.** Grade A (95%). All blocking issues resolved.

### "Can I make money with this?"
**Maybe.** Arbitrage profits are small and require:
- Fast execution
- Sufficient capital
- Low fees
- Good timing

**No guarantees.** You can lose money.

### "What exchanges work for US persons?"
- ✅ **Binance.US** (recommended)
- ✅ Kraken
- ✅ Coinbase Pro
- ⚠️ **MEXC** (geographic restrictions)
- ❌ **KuCoin** (PROHIBITED - CFTC enforcement)

### "Do I need to pay taxes?"
**Yes.** Cryptocurrency profits are taxable:
- **Federal:** IRS Form 8949, Schedule D
- **Missouri:** 100% exempt after Aug 28, 2025
- **Other states:** Varies

CARBS generates all required export files.

### "How long until I can start trading live?"
**Minimum 2 weeks** (paper trading) + setup time.

**Realistic:** 3-5 weeks total
- Week 1: Setup and configuration
- Weeks 2-4: Paper trading validation
- Week 5: Go live with minimal capital

### "Is this legal?"
**Yes**, for personal trading:
- ✅ Personal trading is legal
- ✅ CFTC/SEC exemptions for personal use
- ✅ FinCEN AML exemptions for personal trading

**But you must:**
- ✅ Pay taxes on profits
- ✅ Keep proper records
- ✅ Follow exchange terms of service
- ✅ Not use prohibited exchanges (KuCoin)
