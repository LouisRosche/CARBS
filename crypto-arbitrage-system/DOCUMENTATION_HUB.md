## 🔍 Quick Reference

### Critical Dates
- **Aug 28, 2025:** Missouri capital gains tax exemption effective
- **Jan 1, 2025:** Form 1099-DA reporting begins (gross proceeds only)
- **Jan 1, 2026:** Form 1099-DA cost basis requirement begins

### Key Limits (Default Config)
- **Max Position:** $500 USD
- **Max Daily Loss:** $100 USD
- **Max Daily Trades:** 20
- **Min Spread:** 0.3%

### Exchange Status (for US persons)
- ✅ **Binance.US** - Supported, compliant
- ⚠️ **MEXC** - Geographic restrictions (see docs)
- ❌ **KuCoin** - PROHIBITED (CFTC/FinCEN enforcement)
